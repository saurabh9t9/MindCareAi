Problem

Many young people face stress silently.

Lack of mental health support among youth often leads to stress, anxiety, irregular sleep, and emotional exhaustion. Students frequently hesitate to seek help due to stigma, lack of awareness, or no immediate access to a counselor.

A simple, accessible check-in can make emotional well-being more visible and encourage early intervention before conditions worsen.

Solution

AI wellness check-ins with compassionate guidance

🧠

Smart screening

Users answer quick questions about stress, sleep, mood, and daily wellbeing signals.

💬

Guided chatbot

MannMitra offers supportive conversation, calming exercises, and short self-checks. The demo works offline by default.

📊

Early detection

Patterns help identify when a person may need more support or check-in follow-ups.
